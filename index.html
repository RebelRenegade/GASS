Abstract

Traditional deflationary tokens rely strictly on trading volume to trigger supply burns. During market consolidation or low volume periods, burn mechanics stagnate. $GASS solves this by decoupling tokenomics from market volume and pegging deflation directly to a real-world macroeconomic metric: national retail gasoline prices.

When gas prices rise at the pump, consumer purchasing power declines. $GASS acts as an algorithmic macroeconomic hedge—automatically burning token supply from dedicated reserve accounts whenever real-world gasoline prices break baseline thresholds.

The Oracle Burn Protocol & Simulator

An off-chain automated relayer continuously monitors real-time US national average gasoline price APIs:

  • • Baseline Target: Established at $4.50/gal.
  • • Trigger Mechanics: For every $0.10 increase in retail gas prices, the protocol triggers a 2% Treasury burn .
  • • Source Account: Tokens are permanently destroyed directly out of the 50% Treasury Reserve.
  • • Maximum Burn: At $9.50/gal, all 50 increments above the $4.50 baseline have been reached, resulting in the potential destruction of the entire 500,000,000-token Treasury Reserve.
  • • Holder Impact: Public wallet balances remain completely untouched while global supply shrinks.
